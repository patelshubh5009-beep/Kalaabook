Privacy Policy

Pre-launch draft — review before publishing.

What we collect

Customers provide a name, WhatsApp number, city and preferred service. Artists may also provide a business/display name, area, experience, languages, service groups, travel distance, general map pin, Instagram details, introduction and work photos.

Why we use it

We use registration details to review applications, contact you about your registration and launch updates through WhatsApp, and plan services by city. Registration is not a booking.

Storage and photographs

Registration data is stored using Supabase; the site is hosted through Cloudflare. Uploaded work photos use public storage URLs and can be viewed by anyone who obtains the link, including before profile approval. Do not upload private or sensitive images. External fonts, maps and libraries may receive technical connection information when loaded.

Your choices and contact

To request a correction, deletion, withdrawal of contact consent, or help with a privacy concern, message KalaaBook at @kalaa.book on Instagram or reply to a WhatsApp message received from our team. Please do not send passwords, identity documents or sensitive information through social messages. We may need to verify the request before acting. A dedicated privacy contact and retention schedule must be finalized before public launch.

Children and photo permission

Adults must register on behalf of children. Do not submit a child’s contact details, exact home location, or identifiable photos. Share only work you own or have permission to share.
