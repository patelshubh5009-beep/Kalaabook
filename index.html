Privacy

KalaaBook collects only what you give us in our forms: your name, WhatsApp number, city and what you are looking for or offer. Artists and salons may also give a salon name, Instagram ID, a map pin (an area, not an exact home), how far they travel, a logo and up to 10 work photos.

Details are stored securely with our service provider, Supabase. We show artists' photos on their profiles only because they told us they own them or have permission. A name and city appear on the public list only if the artist ticks that box.

Why

To contact you about KalaaBook, including WhatsApp updates you agreed to, and to plan where we open first.

Children

We do not want a child's name, photo or details. A parent or guardian registers on a child's behalf.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time by replying to any message we send you.
